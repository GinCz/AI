# Pollen-Food Allergy Cross-Reactivity & Diagnostic Matrix

| Primary Pollen Sensitizer | Major Component | Cross-Reactive Food Groups | Clinical Syndrome | Stability / Cooking Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meadow Grasses** (*Phleum pratense*) | `Phl p 1`, `Phl p 5` | **Cereal grains** (Rye, Wheat, Oats, Barley), Tomatoes, Melons, Oranges, Peaches | Pollen-Food Allergy Syndrome (OAS) / GI symptoms | Heat labile (cooking often reduces allergenicity); raw triggers mucosal itching |
| **Birch Pollen** (*Betula verrucosa*) | `Bet v 1` (PR-10) | Apples, Hazelnuts, Carrots, Celery, Cherries, Pears, Soy | Classic Oral Allergy Syndrome (itching/edema of lips, palate, pharynx) | Denatured by cooking / pasteurization |
| **Profilins** (Panallergens) | `Bet v 2`, `Phl p 12` | Broad reactivity: Kiwi, Banana, Melon, Pineapple, Tomato | Mild OAS across multiple unrelated plant families | Highly heat labile |
| **Lipid Transfer Proteins (LTPs)** | `Pru p 3` (Peach) | Stone fruits, Nuts, Grapes, Wheat (`Tri a 19`) | Systemic allergic reactions, FDEIA (anaphylaxis) | **Heat and acid stable** (resists cooking and gastric digestion) |
