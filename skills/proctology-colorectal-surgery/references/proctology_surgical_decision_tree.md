# Proctology Surgical & Interventional Decision Matrix

| Grade / Pathology | Clinical Presentation | First-Line Management | Procedural / Surgical Option | Recovery Time | Pain Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade I Internal** | Painless bleeding, no prolapse | High fiber (Psyllium), MPFF (Diosmin 500-1000 mg), Proktis-M | Sclerotherapy or Infrared Coagulation (IRC) | Immediate | None |
| **Grade II Internal** | Bleeding, prolapse with spontaneous reduction | Fiber, fluid, toilet posture, MPFF (Diosmin) | **Rubber Band Ligation (Barron)** (1-2 cushions/session) | 1-2 days | Mild dull ache (1-2 days) |
| **Grade III Internal** | Prolapse requiring manual digital reduction, bleeding | MPFF, fiber (supportive only) | **Longo Stapled Hemorrhoidopexy (PPH)** or **HAL-RAR** | 5-7 days | Mild to moderate |
| **Grade IV Internal** | Fixed irreducible prolapse, mucus, hygiene compromise | Stool softeners, analgesia (pre-op) | **Excisional Hemorrhoidectomy** (Milligan-Morgan / Ferguson) or Longo (selected) | 3-6 weeks | High (requires scheduled analgesia) |
| **Acute External Thrombosis** | Severe acute pain $\le 72\text{ h}$, blue firm nodule | Ice, rest, NSAIDs | **Surgical Excision / Thrombectomy** under local anesthesia | 3-5 days | Immediate relief of pressure pain |
| **Chronic Anal Fissure** | Severe tearing pain during/after defecation, sentinel tag | Diltiazem 2% / Glyceryl Trinitrate (GTN) 0.4% ointment | Lateral Internal Sphincterotomy (LIS) or Botulinum Toxin injection | 1-2 weeks | Rapid relief of spasm |
