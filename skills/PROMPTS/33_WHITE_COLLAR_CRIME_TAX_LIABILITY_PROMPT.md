   - Czech Republic (§ 240 TZ Zkrácení daně): Damage thresholds (nikoli nepatrná ≥100k CZK, značná ≥1M CZK, velkého rozsahu ≥10M CZK); sanctions up to 10-12 years; § 241 TZ failure to remit withheld taxes/social security; § 242 TZ Účinná lítost (effective repentance extinguishing liability upon full voluntary payment before first-instance verdict).

2. Accounting Violations & Falsification of Books:
   - Czech Republic (§ 254 TZ Zkreslování údajů o stavu hospodaření): Criminalizing non-keeping of statutory books, false entries, or destruction of accounting records; administrative fines under Act on Accounting up to 3-6% of assets and risk of s.r.o. dissolution under § 105 ZOK.
   - Russian Federation: Art. 15.11 KoAP RF (gross violation of accounting rules, >10% tax understatement or report distortion, disqualification of director/accountant); Art. 172.1 UK RF (falsification of financial institution records).

   - Czech Republic (TOPO č. 418/2011 Sb.): Imputation of executive acts to the legal entity; § 8 odst. 5 TOPO Compliance Defense (complete exoneration if company proves all reasonably expected measures were taken: written compliance program, training, whistleblowing channel, four-eyes principle); § 220 TZ and § 51-53 ZOK breach of fiduciary duty (péče řádného hospodáře).
   - Russian Federation: Subsidiary liability of controlling persons (КДЛ) under Arts. 61.11-61.12 Bankruptcy Law No. 127-FZ and Plenum Resolution No. 53; personal unlimited liability for corporate tax debts upon failure to transfer accounting documentation; damages claim against executive under Art. 53.1 GK RF.
4. Tax Inspections & Procedural Defense:
   - Czech Republic (Daňový řád č. 280/2009 Sb.): Místní šetření (§ 80) vs formal Daňová kontrola (§ 85); shifting of burden of proof (§ 92 DŘ obrácené důkazní břemeno); doctrine of abuse of rights (Zneužití práva); administrative appeals and judicial review (§ 65 s.ř.s.).
   - Russian Federation (Art. 54.1 NK RF): Limits on tax deduction rights; business splitting schemes (дробление бизнеса criteria: common founders, IP/MAC, staff, premises); tax reconstruction doctrine; pre-trial mandatory administrative appeals (Art. 138 NK RF) and Arbitrazh court defense.
