# Cardiology Medications Reference & Clinical Pearls

Key cardiovascular drug classes, standard dosages, contraindications, and monitoring parameters.

---

## 1. Antihypertensive & Heart Failure Drug Classes

| Class | Drug Name | Standard Target Dosing | Key Contraindications | Essential Monitoring |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ACE Inhibitor** | Ramipril<br>Perindopril | 2.5–10 mg once daily<br>2.5–10 mg once daily | Pregnancy, history of angioedema, bilateral renal artery stenosis. | Serum Creatinine, eGFR, Potassium ($K^+$). Discontinue if dry cough develops (bradykinin accumulation). |
| **ARB** | Telmisartan<br>Losartan | 40–80 mg once daily<br>50–100 mg once daily | Pregnancy, severe hepatic impairment, bilateral renal artery stenosis. | Serum Creatinine, Potassium. Does not cause bradykinin cough. |
| **ARNI** | Sacubitril / Valsartan (Entresto) | 24/26 mg -> 49/51 mg -> 97/103 mg twice daily | History of angioedema; must allow 36h washout period after stopping ACEi. | Blood pressure (potent vasodilator), renal function, Potassium. |
| **Beta-Blocker** | Bisoprolol<br>Metoprolol Succinate | 1.25–10 mg once daily<br>25–200 mg once daily (ER) | Symptomatic bradycardia ($HR < 50$), 2nd/3rd degree AV block, acute decompensated HF, severe asthma. | Heart rate, blood pressure, ECG PR interval. Never discontinue abruptly (rebound tachycardia). |
| **DHP-CCB** | Amlodipine | 5–10 mg once daily | Severe aortic stenosis, cardiogenic shock. | Lower extremity peripheral edema. Does not depress heart rate or AV conduction. |
| **MRA** | Spironolactone<br>Eplerenone | 25–50 mg once daily<br>25–50 mg once daily | Hyperkalemia ($K^+ > 5.0\text{ mmol/L}$), severe renal failure ($\text{eGFR} < 30$). | Monitor $K^+$ closely (spironolactone can cause painful gynecomastia; eplerenone is selective). |
| **SGLT2 Inhibitor** | Dapagliflozin<br>Empagliflozin | 10 mg once daily<br>10 mg once daily | Severe eGFR reduction ($\text{eGFR} < 20-25$), history of euglycemic DKA. | Genital mycotic infections, hydration status. Reduces cardiovascular mortality in HFrEF. |

## 2. Antithrombotic & Anticoagulant Protocols

| Drug | Type | Indication | Dosing Standard | Laboratory Monitoring |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aspirin (ASA)** | COX-1 Inhibitor | Secondary prevention, CAD, post-PCI | 75–100 mg once daily | Bleeding time, gastroprotection (PPI if high GI risk). |
| **Ticagrelor** | P2Y12 Inhibitor | ACS, post-PCI (DAPT) | 90 mg twice daily | Dyspnea (common, usually self-limiting), bleeding. |
| **Clopidogrel** | P2Y12 Inhibitor | Post-PCI, PAD, ASA allergy | 75 mg once daily | Prodrug requiring CYP2C19 activation; avoid omeprazole co-administration. |
| **Apixaban (Eliquis)** | Factor Xa Inhibitor (DOAC) | Atrial fibrillation, DVT/PE | 5 mg twice daily (reduce to 2.5 mg BID if 2 of: age $\ge 80$, weight $\le 60\text{ kg}$, Cr $\ge 133\ \mu\text{mol/L}$) | No routine coagulation monitoring; check renal function annually. |
| **Warfarin** | Vitamin K Antagonist | Mechanical heart valves, antiphospholipid syndrome | Titrated to target INR | Target INR: **2.0–3.0** (standard AF/bioprosthetic) or **2.5–3.5** (mechanical mitral valve). |
