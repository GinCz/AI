# Knee Rehabilitation & Therapeutic Exercise Guide

An operational clinical reference for conservative knee joint strengthening and post-arthroscopic recovery.

---

## 1. Acute Phase / Conservative Restoration Exercises

### Exercise 1: Isometric Quadriceps Sets (Quad Sets)
- **Position**: Supine or long-sitting on a firm surface, legs straight.
- **Action**: Tighten the quadriceps muscle on the front of the thigh, pushing the back of the knee down firmly into the mat/bed. Place a small rolled towel under the ankle to encourage full hyperextension.
- **Dose**: Hold contraction for 5–10 seconds. Repeat 3 sets of 15 repetitions, 3 times daily.
- **Goal**: Combat quadriceps arthrogenic muscle inhibition caused by joint effusion.

### Exercise 2: Straight Leg Raise (SLR)
- **Position**: Supine, contralateral knee bent to $90^\circ$ with foot flat on floor.
- **Action**: Fully contract the quadriceps of the involved leg into complete extension, then raise the leg 20–30 cm off the surface while keeping the knee locked straight (no extension lag).
- **Dose**: Hold for 3 seconds at top, lower slowly. 3 sets of 10–12 repetitions.
- **Goal**: Anterior chain strengthening without patellofemoral compressive joint forces.

### Exercise 3: Terminal Knee Extension (TKE)
- **Position**: Standing facing an anchor point, with an elastic resistance band looped behind the knee crease (*fossa poplitea*).
- **Action**: Start with knee slightly bent ($20^\circ$), then contract the quadriceps to pull the knee into full straight extension against band resistance.
- **Dose**: 3 sets of 15 repetitions.
- **Goal**: Selective activation of the Vastus Medialis Oblique (VMO) at the final $15^\circ$ of extension.

## 2. Dynamic Stability & Patellofemoral Protection

### Exercise 4: Clamshells & Side-Lying Hip Abduction
- **Position**: Side-lying with hips flexed $45^\circ$, knees flexed $90^\circ$.
- **Action**: Keep feet glued together; lift the top knee toward the ceiling without rotating the pelvis backward.
- **Goal**: Gluteus medius strengthening to eliminate dynamic knee valgus (inward knee collapse) during walking and stairs.

### Exercise 5: Closed-Kinetic Chain Mini-Squats / Wall Slides
- **Position**: Standing with back flat against a smooth wall, feet shoulder-width apart, 30 cm out from the wall.
- **Action**: Slide down the wall until knees reach **maximum $45^\circ–60^\circ$ flexion** (do NOT exceed $60^\circ$ to avoid high patellofemoral cartilage peak stress).
- **Dose**: Hold for 5 seconds, slide back up. 3 sets of 10 repetitions.
